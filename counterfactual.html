What blocked completion

The task-5 control replays remained stuck on identity collection. In corrected runs that found a customer, reaching the account did not complete the requested order actions. Some responses promised to search or act without issuing the next tool call. Frozen caller speech also cannot supply new details or confirmations in response to a changed dialogue.

How the comparison worked

Both arms used fresh sessions of the same saved Nemotron Blueprint image, the same recorded voice-agent instructions and retail tools, and independent copies of the same native database. All saved final-ASR events were replayed in order. Only the listed word changed. Arms were repeated three times with alternating order; all trajectories received the unchanged native retail checks, with GPT-5.5 judging where the task requires it.

How to read the result

A successful customer lookup is an intermediate improvement. A task pass requires the complete requested outcome. The saved caller recordings often stop before supplying the remaining details or confirmations, so these replay scores cannot predict whether an adaptive live caller would finish the task. No LM has been trained.

Limits of the test

  • Transcript-only replay runs serialized turns, not original audio delivery, wall-clock finalization schedule, interruption, or TTS playback. It is not the native voice success rate.
  • Saved caller speech cannot adapt to new assistant responses, and may lack details/confirmations never reached in the historical failure.
  • Name spellings are conditional machine-checked corrections, not human-verified golden references.
  • Task 5 early Kovac-to-Kovacs correction is excluded because blind audio verifiers disagree about the final s.
  • No GPT-5.5 caller simulation or Gemini TTS is run: the exact saved ASR text is held fixed.
  • A score remaining zero does not prove ASR had no contribution; a replay score rescue does not prove an ASR LM could produce the correction.
