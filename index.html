What still blocked completion

  • 037, corrected, repetition 1: found the customer and order and answered both price requirements, but made no required order change. Language requirements passed; the database check failed.
  • 037, corrected, repetition 2: found the account, but cancelled the whole order instead of making the required item changes. The database check failed.
  • 3 corrected calls received a name fix but executed no retail tools. Better name text alone did not resolve those stalls.

These are observed outcomes, not proof of a single cause. The sample is small; the retrieval-count difference does not establish a reliable causal benefit. This tests selected name forms, not perfect ASR or a trained LM. Full live-comparison method and limits.

Earlier baseline: WER remains provisional

14 retail tasks × two speech presets = 28 baseline conversations; 2 passed. Provisional pooled WER was 8.41% (318 errors / 3,781 words) across 26 complete machine references. There are zero human-certified golden references. Task 035 regular has a verifier-filter gap; 037 regular has unresolved inaudible speech. Their full WER is withheld.

The targeted subset covered names, spelling, emails, digits, negation and background speech when errors plausibly affected task completion. Six tasks were for development and eight for evaluation, with separate identities. The initial trajectory review identified three likely ASR contributions among 26 failures; this was a qualitative judgment, not three demonstrated rescues. Earlier frozen-text replays also produced no task passes. Initial review · Text replay.

The baseline and live comparison are separate experiments; their counts are not pooled. No new live-run WER or LM-on/off WER has been calculated.

Audio and experiment checks

Exact delivered audio was matched to final ASR events; interim guesses were excluded. All nine applied name replacements reached the assistant. Twenty targeted audio clips have saved blind Gemini and GPT Transcribe checks. Machine agreement does not resolve every name spelling or create human-certified gold.

Recommended next steps

  1. For task success: fix tool execution, identifier handling, order-change behavior and voice-flow stalls before expanding task-rescue claims.
  2. For ASR: complete human reference review. Any later LM trial should use separate domain text and the same frozen audio for LM-off/on WER and identifier checks, followed by live task and other-domain comparisons. Keep selected POC tasks, identities and references out of training.
